1c:T1f23,<p>Oh My Zsh 的外掛系統是它讓人留下來用的核心原因——但很多人裝了之後只開了 <code>git</code>，然後就沒有然後了。</p>
<p>這篇把外掛系統說清楚：怎麼運作、怎麼啟用、哪些值得裝、第三方外掛怎麼管。</p>
<h2 id="外掛分兩種管法完全不一樣">外掛分兩種，管法完全不一樣</h2>
<p><strong>內建外掛</strong>（bundled plugins）住在 <code>~/.oh-my-zsh/plugins/</code> 目錄裡，裝 OMZ 時就帶進來了，不用另外下載，直接在 <code>.zshrc</code> 的 <code>plugins=()</code> 加名字就啟用。</p>
<p><strong>第三方外掛</strong>（community plugins）不在上面那個目錄裡，你要自己 clone 到 <code>$ZSH_CUSTOM/plugins/</code> 底下，然後才能在 <code>plugins=()</code> 加名字。</p>
<p>這個差異不搞清楚，就會出現「我明明加了名字，為什麼指令還是沒用」的困惑。</p>
<h2 id="啟用內建外掛">啟用內建外掛</h2>
<p>打開 <code>~/.zshrc</code>，找到 <code>plugins</code> 那行：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">plugins</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> z</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> docker</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> npm</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> node</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>括號內用空白分隔，不用引號。改完記得 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code>。</p>
<p>幾個值得加進去的內建外掛：</p>

<p><code>sudo</code> 那個是我私心推薦的——下了指令發現需要權限，按兩下 Esc 就搞定，不用重打整行。</p>
<h2 id="安裝第三方外掛">安裝第三方外掛</h2>
<p>有兩個第三方外掛幾乎是必裝的：<code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 和 <code>zsh-syntax-highlighting</code>。裝法都一樣：</p>
<p><strong>1. clone 到 custom plugins 目錄</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> clone</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-autosuggestions</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> \</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">  ${ZSH_CUSTOM</span><span style="color:#F97583">:-</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">~</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">.oh-my-zsh</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">custom}</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> clone</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-syntax-highlighting</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> \</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">  ${ZSH_CUSTOM</span><span style="color:#F97583">:-</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">~</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">.oh-my-zsh</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">custom}</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">/plugins/zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>2. 加到 <code>.zshrc</code> 的 plugins 清單</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">plugins</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  git</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  z</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  sudo</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>注意：<code>zsh-syntax-highlighting</code> 要放在 plugins 清單的<strong>最後一個</strong>，否則語法高亮可能失效。</p>
<p><strong>3. 重載設定</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">source</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> ~/.zshrc</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h2 id="zsh-syntax-highlighting-做了什麼">zsh-syntax-highlighting 做了什麼</h2>
<p>打指令時，有效的指令顯示綠色，打錯的顯示紅色——在按下 Enter 之前就知道有沒有拼錯。</p>
<p>聽起來很小，但你踩過「打了一長串指令按 Enter 才發現第一個字打錯」的痛，就知道這個多有價值。</p>
<h2 id="zsh-autosuggestions-的故事">zsh-autosuggestions 的故事</h2>
<p>這個外掛值得獨立一篇來說——它根據歷史記錄即時預測你下一步要打什麼，而且設定空間非常大。</p>
<p>下一篇 <a href="03-auto-suggestions">03-auto-suggestions</a> 完整深挖：預測策略、補完快捷鍵、效能調校、以及讓它在 ssh 環境也能好好運作的設定。</p>
<h2 id="外掛太多會變慢">外掛太多會變慢</h2>
<p>這是真實存在的問題——每個外掛在 shell 啟動時都要跑一段 script。裝太多的話，<code>zsh</code> 啟動會開始感覺到延遲。</p>
<p>判斷標準很簡單，跑一下：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#F97583">time</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> zsh -i -c exit</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>結果超過 <strong>1 秒</strong>就該注意了。超過 <strong>2 秒</strong>肯定是哪個外掛在拖後腿，用二分法逐一停用找出來。</p>
<p>一般來說，外掛清單控制在 10 個以內比較保險。</p>

28:T1f23,<p>Oh My Zsh 的外掛系統是它讓人留下來用的核心原因——但很多人裝了之後只開了 <code>git</code>，然後就沒有然後了。</p>
<p>這篇把外掛系統說清楚：怎麼運作、怎麼啟用、哪些值得裝、第三方外掛怎麼管。</p>
<h2 id="外掛分兩種管法完全不一樣">外掛分兩種，管法完全不一樣</h2>
<p><strong>內建外掛</strong>（bundled plugins）住在 <code>~/.oh-my-zsh/plugins/</code> 目錄裡，裝 OMZ 時就帶進來了，不用另外下載，直接在 <code>.zshrc</code> 的 <code>plugins=()</code> 加名字就啟用。</p>
<p><strong>第三方外掛</strong>（community plugins）不在上面那個目錄裡，你要自己 clone 到 <code>$ZSH_CUSTOM/plugins/</code> 底下，然後才能在 <code>plugins=()</code> 加名字。</p>
<p>這個差異不搞清楚，就會出現「我明明加了名字，為什麼指令還是沒用」的困惑。</p>
<h2 id="啟用內建外掛">啟用內建外掛</h2>
<p>打開 <code>~/.zshrc</code>，找到 <code>plugins</code> 那行：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">plugins</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> z</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> docker</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> npm</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> node</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>括號內用空白分隔，不用引號。改完記得 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code>。</p>
<p>幾個值得加進去的內建外掛：</p>

<p><code>sudo</code> 那個是我私心推薦的——下了指令發現需要權限，按兩下 Esc 就搞定，不用重打整行。</p>
<h2 id="安裝第三方外掛">安裝第三方外掛</h2>
<p>有兩個第三方外掛幾乎是必裝的：<code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 和 <code>zsh-syntax-highlighting</code>。裝法都一樣：</p>
<p><strong>1. clone 到 custom plugins 目錄</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> clone</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-autosuggestions</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> \</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">  ${ZSH_CUSTOM</span><span style="color:#F97583">:-</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">~</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">.oh-my-zsh</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">custom}</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> clone</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-syntax-highlighting</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> \</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">  ${ZSH_CUSTOM</span><span style="color:#F97583">:-</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">~</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">.oh-my-zsh</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">custom}</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">/plugins/zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>2. 加到 <code>.zshrc</code> 的 plugins 清單</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">plugins</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  git</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  z</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  sudo</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  zsh-autosuggestions</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  zsh-syntax-highlighting</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>注意：<code>zsh-syntax-highlighting</code> 要放在 plugins 清單的<strong>最後一個</strong>，否則語法高亮可能失效。</p>
<p><strong>3. 重載設定</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">source</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> ~/.zshrc</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h2 id="zsh-syntax-highlighting-做了什麼">zsh-syntax-highlighting 做了什麼</h2>
<p>打指令時，有效的指令顯示綠色，打錯的顯示紅色——在按下 Enter 之前就知道有沒有拼錯。</p>
<p>聽起來很小，但你踩過「打了一長串指令按 Enter 才發現第一個字打錯」的痛，就知道這個多有價值。</p>
<h2 id="zsh-autosuggestions-的故事">zsh-autosuggestions 的故事</h2>
<p>這個外掛值得獨立一篇來說——它根據歷史記錄即時預測你下一步要打什麼，而且設定空間非常大。</p>
<p>下一篇 <a href="03-auto-suggestions">03-auto-suggestions</a> 完整深挖：預測策略、補完快捷鍵、效能調校、以及讓它在 ssh 環境也能好好運作的設定。</p>
<h2 id="外掛太多會變慢">外掛太多會變慢</h2>
<p>這是真實存在的問題——每個外掛在 shell 啟動時都要跑一段 script。裝太多的話，<code>zsh</code> 啟動會開始感覺到延遲。</p>
<p>判斷標準很簡單，跑一下：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#F97583">time</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> zsh -i -c exit</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>結果超過 <strong>1 秒</strong>就該注意了。超過 <strong>2 秒</strong>肯定是哪個外掛在拖後腿，用二分法逐一停用找出來。</p>
<p>一般來說，外掛清單控制在 10 個以內比較保險。</p>
