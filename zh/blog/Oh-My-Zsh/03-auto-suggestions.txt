1d:T49ac,<p>你每天打最多次的指令，可能就是那十幾二十個——<code>git pull</code>、<code>docker-compose up -d</code>、<code>kubectl get pods -n prod</code>。</p>
<p>每次都要完整打完，有點浪費。</p>
<p><strong>zsh-autosuggestions</strong> 做的事情是：根據你的歷史記錄，在你打字的時候用灰色文字預測後面的內容——你覺得對了，按一個鍵直接補完，不用完整打完。</p>
<p>用過三天之後，你會發現自己回到沒裝的環境，手指會停在那邊等一下——因為預測沒出來。</p>

<h2 id="安裝">安裝</h2>
<p>如果已經跟著 <a href="02-plugins">02-plugins</a> 裝好了，這段可以跳過。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> clone</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-autosuggestions</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> \</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">  ${ZSH_CUSTOM</span><span style="color:#F97583">:-</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">~</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">.oh-my-zsh</span><span style="color:#F97583">/</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">custom}</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>然後在 <code>~/.zshrc</code> 的 <code>plugins=()</code> 加進去：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">plugins</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">git</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> z</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> sudo</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> zsh-autosuggestions</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> zsh-syntax-highlighting</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">source</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> ~/.zshrc</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>裝完之後開始打指令，灰色文字就會出現了。</p>
<h2 id="補完快捷鍵">補完快捷鍵</h2>
<p>預設是按右方向鍵 <code>→</code> 補完整行建議。對大多數人夠用，但如果你習慣用方向鍵左右移動游標，可能會衝突——這種情況就要自訂。</p>
<p><strong>補完整行（預設）</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># ~/.zshrc</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '→'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>補完單個字</strong>（只接受下一個 word）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^→'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> forward-word</span><span style="color:#6A737D">   # Ctrl+右鍵</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>用 End 鍵補完</strong>（某些人偏好）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> "${</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">terminfo</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">[kend]}"</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>把 Ctrl+F 設為補完</strong>（不影響方向鍵）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^F'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個我用了一段時間，Ctrl+F 原本在 Vim 裡是 Page Down，但在 Zsh 裡沒有衝突，補完手感很順。</p>
<p>改完一樣 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code>，馬上生效。</p>
<h2 id="預測策略">預測策略</h2>
<p><code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 有兩種找建議的策略，可以單獨用，也可以組合：</p>
<h3 id="history預設"><code>history</code>（預設）</h3>
<p>從你的歷史記錄裡找最近一次匹配的指令。簡單、快、多數情境夠用。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h3 id="match_prev_cmd"><code>match_prev_cmd</code></h3>
<p>除了看你打的前綴，還會考慮你<strong>上一條指令</strong>是什麼——根據脈絡預測你現在要打什麼。</p>
<p>舉個例子：你剛打完 <code>git add .</code>，接著開始打 <code>git</code>，它會優先建議 <code>git commit -m "..."</code> 而不是其他 git 指令，因為 add 後面最常見的就是 commit。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">match_prev_cmd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>陣列的順序就是優先順序，<code>match_prev_cmd</code> 找不到才 fallback 到 <code>history</code>。</p>
<h3 id="completion實驗性"><code>completion</code>（實驗性）</h3>
<p>直接呼叫 Zsh 的補完引擎來生成建議，不是從歷史裡找。這個比較吃效能，啟動和補完都會感覺慢一點——除非有特殊需求，建議先不開。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">history</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> completion</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)  </span><span style="color:#6A737D"># 組合使用</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h2 id="建議文字顏色">建議文字顏色</h2>
<p>預設的灰色在深色主題下通常沒問題，但淺色主題可能看不清楚，或是跟你的 Powerlevel10k 顏色搭不好。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># ~/.zshrc，加在 plugins 之前的某處</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=244"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">          # 淺灰，深色主題推薦</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=8"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">            # 暗灰，更保守</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=#666666"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">      # 直接指定 hex（需終端支援 truecolor）</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=244,underline"</span><span style="color:#6A737D"> # 底線版，更明顯</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>顏色編號 <code>fg=0</code> 到 <code>fg=255</code> 對應標準 256 色終端。如果你不確定哪個好看，<code>fg=244</code> 是最安全的選項。</p>
<h2 id="讓某些情況自動清除建議">讓某些情況自動清除建議</h2>
<p>有時你打完一條指令、按下 Enter 之後，下一行冒出來的建議是完全無關的東西——讓版面變亂。可以設定哪些操作會自動清掉當前建議：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_CLEAR_WIDGETS</span><span style="color:#F97583">+=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-backward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-forward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個設定告訴它：當你用歷史搜尋在翻指令時，清掉當前建議。不然翻歷史的時候，建議文字和游標之間會出現奇怪的覆蓋。</p>
<h2 id="效能非同步模式">效能：非同步模式</h2>
<p>Zsh 5.0.8 以上的版本，<code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 預設就是用<strong>非同步方式</strong>找建議——找建議的過程不會卡住你打字。</p>
<p>如果你的 Zsh 比較舊，或是想確認有沒有開啟：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_USE_ASYNC</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">1</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>另外，歷史記錄如果非常大（幾萬條），搜尋建議本身也可能變慢。這個可以限制建議搜尋的最大字元長度，避免對超長指令做完整匹配：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_BUFFER_MAX_SIZE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">20</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>超過 20 個字元的輸入就不顯示建議，這樣對長指令的效能影響降到最低。</p>
<h2 id="常見問題">常見問題</h2>
<p><strong>建議文字不出現</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>確認 <code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 有在 <code>plugins=()</code> 裡</li>
<li>確認有 clone 到 <code>${ZSH_CUSTOM}/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions</code></li>
<li>跑 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code> 重載</li>
<li>打幾條指令建立歷史記錄再試——新安裝的 Zsh 歷史記錄是空的，沒有東西可以建議</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>tmux 裡建議顏色變怪</strong></p>
<p>tmux 的顏色支援和終端機本身不同，可能把 <code>fg=244</code> 算錯。試試改成更基礎的：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=8"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>或確認 tmux 設定裡有：</p>
<pre><code>set -g default-terminal "screen-256color"
set -ag terminal-overrides ",xterm-256color:RGB"
</code></pre>
<p><strong>SSH 連線裡建議補完沒反應</strong></p>
<p>這通常是 <code>bindkey</code> 的問題——SSH 終端機傳過來的 key code 可能不一樣。確認你的 <code>bindkey</code> 設定，或改用字符而不是 <code>terminfo</code> 對應：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^F'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span><span style="color:#6A737D">   # Ctrl+F，比較不受終端機影響</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>建議一直跳到我不想要的指令</strong></p>
<p>歷史記錄裡有一些你很久以前打的長指令一直被預測出來，但現在用不到。清一下歷史：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 直接編輯歷史檔案（通常在 ~/.zsh_history）</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 刪掉你不想要的行</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 或用這個指令搜尋並確認刪除</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">fc</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> -R</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> &#x26;&#x26; </span><span style="color:#79B8FF">history</span><span style="color:#F97583"> |</span><span style="color:#B392F0"> grep</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> "那個煩人的指令"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h2 id="完整設定範例">完整設定範例</h2>
<p>把以下內容貼到 <code>~/.zshrc</code> 的外掛載入之後（<code>source $ZSH/oh-my-zsh.sh</code> 之後）：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-autosuggestions 設定</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=244"</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">match_prev_cmd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_BUFFER_MAX_SIZE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">20</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_USE_ASYNC</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">1</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 補完快捷鍵</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^F'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span><span style="color:#6A737D">          # Ctrl+F 補完整行</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^[→'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> forward-word</span><span style="color:#6A737D">               # Alt+右鍵 補完單字（macOS 可能要另外設定）</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 翻歷史時清掉建議</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_CLEAR_WIDGETS</span><span style="color:#F97583">+=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-backward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-forward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>存檔、<code>source ~/.zshrc</code>，打幾條指令讓歷史有東西，然後試試看。</p>

<h2 id="安裝">安裝</h2>
<p>如果已經跟著 <a href="02-plugins">02-plugins</a> 裝好了，這段可以跳過。</p>

<h2 id="補完快捷鍵">補完快捷鍵</h2>
<p>預設是按右方向鍵 <code>→</code> 補完整行建議。對大多數人夠用，但如果你習慣用方向鍵左右移動游標，可能會衝突——這種情況就要自訂。</p>
<p><strong>補完整行（預設）</strong></p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># ~/.zshrc</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '→'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>補完單個字</strong>（只接受下一個 word）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^→'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> forward-word</span><span style="color:#6A737D">   # Ctrl+右鍵</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>用 End 鍵補完</strong>（某些人偏好）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> "${</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">terminfo</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">[kend]}"</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>把 Ctrl+F 設為補完</strong>（不影響方向鍵）</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^F'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個我用了一段時間，Ctrl+F 原本在 Vim 裡是 Page Down，但在 Zsh 裡沒有衝突，補完手感很順。</p>
<p>改完一樣 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code>，馬上生效。</p>
<h2 id="預測策略">預測策略</h2>
<p><code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 有兩種找建議的策略，可以單獨用，也可以組合：</p>
<h3 id="history預設"><code>history</code>（預設）</h3>
<p>從你的歷史記錄裡找最近一次匹配的指令。簡單、快、多數情境夠用。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h3 id="match_prev_cmd"><code>match_prev_cmd</code></h3>
<p>除了看你打的前綴，還會考慮你<strong>上一條指令</strong>是什麼——根據脈絡預測你現在要打什麼。</p>
<p>舉個例子：你剛打完 <code>git add .</code>，接著開始打 <code>git</code>，它會優先建議 <code>git commit -m "..."</code> 而不是其他 git 指令，因為 add 後面最常見的就是 commit。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">match_prev_cmd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>陣列的順序就是優先順序，<code>match_prev_cmd</code> 找不到才 fallback 到 <code>history</code>。</p>
<h3 id="completion實驗性"><code>completion</code>（實驗性）</h3>
<p>直接呼叫 Zsh 的補完引擎來生成建議，不是從歷史裡找。這個比較吃效能，啟動和補完都會感覺慢一點——除非有特殊需求，建議先不開。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">history</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> completion</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)  </span><span style="color:#6A737D"># 組合使用</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h2 id="建議文字顏色">建議文字顏色</h2>
<p>預設的灰色在深色主題下通常沒問題，但淺色主題可能看不清楚，或是跟你的 Powerlevel10k 顏色搭不好。</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># ~/.zshrc，加在 plugins 之前的某處</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=244"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">          # 淺灰，深色主題推薦</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=8"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">            # 暗灰，更保守</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=#666666"</span><span style="color:#6A737D">      # 直接指定 hex（需終端支援 truecolor）</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=244,underline"</span><span style="color:#6A737D"> # 底線版，更明顯</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>顏色編號 <code>fg=0</code> 到 <code>fg=255</code> 對應標準 256 色終端。如果你不確定哪個好看，<code>fg=244</code> 是最安全的選項。</p>
<h2 id="讓某些情況自動清除建議">讓某些情況自動清除建議</h2>
<p>有時你打完一條指令、按下 Enter 之後，下一行冒出來的建議是完全無關的東西——讓版面變亂。可以設定哪些操作會自動清掉當前建議：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_CLEAR_WIDGETS</span><span style="color:#F97583">+=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-backward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">  history-beginning-search-forward-end</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個設定告訴它：當你用歷史搜尋在翻指令時，清掉當前建議。不然翻歷史的時候，建議文字和游標之間會出現奇怪的覆蓋。</p>
<h2 id="效能非同步模式">效能：非同步模式</h2>
<p>Zsh 5.0.8 以上的版本，<code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 預設就是用<strong>非同步方式</strong>找建議——找建議的過程不會卡住你打字。</p>
<p>如果你的 Zsh 比較舊，或是想確認有沒有開啟：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_USE_ASYNC</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">1</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>另外，歷史記錄如果非常大（幾萬條），搜尋建議本身也可能變慢。這個可以限制建議搜尋的最大字元長度，避免對超長指令做完整匹配：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_BUFFER_MAX_SIZE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">20</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>超過 20 個字元的輸入就不顯示建議，這樣對長指令的效能影響降到最低。</p>
<h2 id="常見問題">常見問題</h2>
<p><strong>建議文字不出現</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>確認 <code>zsh-autosuggestions</code> 有在 <code>plugins=()</code> 裡</li>
<li>確認有 clone 到 <code>${ZSH_CUSTOM}/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions</code></li>
<li>跑 <code>source ~/.zshrc</code> 重載</li>
<li>打幾條指令建立歷史記錄再試——新安裝的 Zsh 歷史記錄是空的，沒有東西可以建議</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>tmux 裡建議顏色變怪</strong></p>
<p>tmux 的顏色支援和終端機本身不同，可能把 <code>fg=244</code> 算錯。試試改成更基礎的：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_HIGHLIGHT_STYLE</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"fg=8"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>或確認 tmux 設定裡有：</p>
<pre><code>set -g default-terminal "screen-256color"
set -ag terminal-overrides ",xterm-256color:RGB"
</code></pre>
<p><strong>SSH 連線裡建議補完沒反應</strong></p>
<p>這通常是 <code>bindkey</code> 的問題——SSH 終端機傳過來的 key code 可能不一樣。確認你的 <code>bindkey</code> 設定，或改用字符而不是 <code>terminfo</code> 對應：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">bindkey</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> '^F'</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> autosuggest-accept</span><span style="color:#6A737D">   # Ctrl+F，比較不受終端機影響</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><strong>建議一直跳到我不想要的指令</strong></p>
<p>歷史記錄裡有一些你很久以前打的長指令一直被預測出來，但現在用不到。清一下歷史：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 直接編輯歷史檔案（通常在 ~/.zsh_history）</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 刪掉你不想要的行</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># 或用這個指令搜尋並確認刪除</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">fc</span><span style="color:#79B8FF"> -R</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> &#x26;&#x26; </span><span style="color:#79B8FF">history</span><span style="color:#F97583"> |</span><span style="color:#B392F0"> grep</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> "那個煩人的指令"</span></span></code></pre></figure>

<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="zsh" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#6A737D"># zsh-autosuggestions 設定</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ZSH_AUTOSUGGEST_STRATEGY</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">match_prev_cmd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> history</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span>
