1b:T2736,<p>學新工具最容易卡在「還沒看到成果就先被設定地雷炸死」。</p>
<p>這篇的目標只有一個：<strong>你跟著做完，就能跑出第一份 AI 研究報告</strong>。其他進階概念先不談，先讓你看到東西跑起來，有感覺再深入。</p>
<h2 id="建立專案">建立專案</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> create</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> crew</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> latest-ai-notes</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">cd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> latest-ai-notes</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個指令會幫你建好基本骨架，你不用從空目錄開始手刻：</p>
<pre><code>src/latest_ai_notes/
├── config/
│   ├── agents.yaml    ← 定義角色
│   └── tasks.yaml     ← 定義任務
├── crew.py            ← 組裝 Crew
└── main.py            ← 執行入口
</code></pre>
<h2 id="安裝依賴">安裝依賴</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> install</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個指令會自動讀取專案設定、建立虛擬環境、安裝所有套件。不用自己拼一堆 <code>pip install</code>，直接跑就好。</p>
<p>第一次執行會下載模型相關的東西，速度取決於你的網路——端杯咖啡，大約等個 30 秒到 2 分鐘。</p>
<h2 id="設定-agent">設定 Agent</h2>
<p>打開 <code>config/agents.yaml</code>，改成這樣：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">researcher</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  role</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    {topic} Researcher</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  goal</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Find useful and current information about {topic}</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  backstory</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    You are good at collecting reliable information and summarizing key points.</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">reporter</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  role</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    {topic} Reporter</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Convert research notes into a readable markdown report</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  backstory</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    You explain technical topics with simple language and clear structure.</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這裡定義了兩個角色：一個負責找資料，一個負責把資料寫成報告。<code>{topic}</code> 是執行時傳入的變數，後面會看到怎麼用。</p>
<h2 id="設定-task">設定 Task</h2>
<p>打開 <code>config/tasks.yaml</code>，改成這樣：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">research_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  description</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Research {topic}. Focus on trends, tools, and practical examples in 2026.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  expected_output</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    10 bullet points with short explanations.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Each bullet must include: title, summary (1-2 sentences), and source URL if available.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  agent</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">researcher</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">report_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  description</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Expand the research notes into a complete markdown article for beginners.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  expected_output</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    A beginner-friendly markdown report with: introduction, 3 main sections, and conclusion.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Tone: practical and accessible.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  agent</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">reporter</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  context</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    - </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">research_task</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  output_file</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">output/report.md</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><code>context: [research_task]</code> 這行很關鍵——它告訴 <code>report_task</code> 要吃 <code>research_task</code> 的輸出結果。少了這行，reporter 會自己憑空猜測，而不是真的用研究員找到的資料。</p>
<h2 id="執行-crew">執行 Crew</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> run</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>成功的話，<code>output/report.md</code> 會出現你的第一份 AI 生成報告。</p>
<p>第一次跑如果有延遲很正常——模型呼叫就像貓主子起床，要等一下才會理你。通常 30 秒到 2 分鐘內會完成。</p>
<h2 id="跑出來是什麼感覺">跑出來是什麼感覺？</h2>
<p>終端機會顯示每個 Agent 的執行過程，你可以看到研究員在查哪些資料、reporter 怎麼組織報告。<code>verbose=True</code> 預設開啟，這個在除錯時非常有用，先別關掉。</p>
<p>跑完後，<code>output/report.md</code> 裡會有一份結構化的 markdown 報告。第一次看到的感受通常是：「這東西還真的跑起來了。」</p>

<p><strong>Q：可以改換其他主題嗎？</strong><br>
可以。在 <code>main.py</code> 找到 <code>inputs</code> 這個 dict，把 <code>topic</code> 的值改成你想研究的任何東西。</p>
<p><strong>Q：需要 OpenAI 以外的模型嗎？</strong><br>
預設用 GPT-4o，但也支援 Anthropic Claude、Gemini、甚至本地跑的 Ollama。第一次先用 OpenAI 測通，後面換模型比想像中容易。</p>
<p><strong>Q：output 目錄不存在會報錯嗎？</strong><br>
會。記得先建立 <code>output/</code> 目錄，或者在 <code>main.py</code> 加上自動建目錄的邏輯。</p>
<h2 id="下一步">下一步</h2>

<p>這篇的目標只有一個：<strong>你跟著做完，就能跑出第一份 AI 研究報告</strong>。其他進階概念先不談，先讓你看到東西跑起來，有感覺再深入。</p>
<h2 id="建立專案">建立專案</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> create</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> crew</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> latest-ai-notes</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#79B8FF">cd</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> latest-ai-notes</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個指令會幫你建好基本骨架，你不用從空目錄開始手刻：</p>
<pre><code>src/latest_ai_notes/
├── config/
│   ├── agents.yaml    ← 定義角色
│   └── tasks.yaml     ← 定義任務
├── crew.py            ← 組裝 Crew
└── main.py            ← 執行入口
</code></pre>
<h2 id="安裝依賴">安裝依賴</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> install</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這個指令會自動讀取專案設定、建立虛擬環境、安裝所有套件。不用自己拼一堆 <code>pip install</code>，直接跑就好。</p>
<p>第一次執行會下載模型相關的東西，速度取決於你的網路——端杯咖啡，大約等個 30 秒到 2 分鐘。</p>
<h2 id="設定-agent">設定 Agent</h2>
<p>打開 <code>config/agents.yaml</code>，改成這樣：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">researcher</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  role</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    {topic} Researcher</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  goal</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Find useful and current information about {topic}</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  backstory</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    You are good at collecting reliable information and summarizing key points.</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">reporter</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  role</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    {topic} Reporter</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  goal</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Convert research notes into a readable markdown report</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  backstory</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    You explain technical topics with simple language and clear structure.</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>這裡定義了兩個角色：一個負責找資料，一個負責把資料寫成報告。<code>{topic}</code> 是執行時傳入的變數，後面會看到怎麼用。</p>
<h2 id="設定-task">設定 Task</h2>
<p>打開 <code>config/tasks.yaml</code>，改成這樣：</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">research_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  description</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Research {topic}. Focus on trends, tools, and practical examples in 2026.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  expected_output</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    10 bullet points with short explanations.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Each bullet must include: title, summary (1-2 sentences), and source URL if available.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  agent</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">researcher</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">report_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  description</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Expand the research notes into a complete markdown article for beginners.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  expected_output</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    A beginner-friendly markdown report with: introduction, 3 main sections, and conclusion.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Tone: practical and accessible.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  agent</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">reporter</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  context</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    - </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">research_task</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  output_file</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">output/report.md</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p><code>context: [research_task]</code> 這行很關鍵——它告訴 <code>report_task</code> 要吃 <code>research_task</code> 的輸出結果。少了這行，reporter 會自己憑空猜測，而不是真的用研究員找到的資料。</p>
<h2 id="執行-crew">執行 Crew</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> run</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>成功的話，<code>output/report.md</code> 會出現你的第一份 AI 生成報告。</p>
<p>第一次跑如果有延遲很正常——模型呼叫就像貓主子起床，要等一下才會理你。通常 30 秒到 2 分鐘內會完成。</p>
<h2 id="跑出來是什麼感覺">跑出來是什麼感覺？</h2>
<p>終端機會顯示每個 Agent 的執行過程，你可以看到研究員在查哪些資料、reporter 怎麼組織報告。<code>verbose=True</code> 預設開啟，這個在除錯時非常有用，先別關掉。</p>
<p>跑完後，<code>output/report.md</code> 裡會有一份結構化的 markdown 報告。第一次看到的感受通常是：「這東西還真的跑起來了。」</p>

<p><strong>Q：可以改換其他主題嗎？</strong><br>
可以。在 <code>main.py</code> 找到 <code>inputs</code> 這個 dict，把 <code>topic</code> 的值改成你想研究的任何東西。</p>
<p><strong>Q：需要 OpenAI 以外的模型嗎？</strong><br>
預設用 GPT-4o，但也支援 Anthropic Claude、Gemini、甚至本地跑的 Ollama。第一次先用 OpenAI 測通，後面換模型比想像中容易。</p>
<p><strong>Q：output 目錄不存在會報錯嗎？</strong><br>
會。記得先建立 <code>output/</code> 目錄，或者在 <code>main.py</code> 加上自動建目錄的邏輯。</p>
