<p><strong>Who owns the ingress layer? Who can change routes? Can namespaces cross-reference each other?</strong></p>
<p>Many Ingress pain points came from everything being tangled together.
Platform teams feared unauthorized exposure changes. App teams felt like they had to file a ticket just to change a path. Everyone ended up torturing each other in YAML.</p>
<p>Gateway API's approach is to say clearly:</p>
<ul>
<li>Platform ingress can be independently governed</li>
<li>Routes can be delegated to app teams</li>
<li>Cross-namespace behavior requires explicit authorization</li>
</ul>
<p>This post explains that ownership model from top to bottom.</p>
<h2 id="start-here-attachment-and-reference-are-two-different-things">Start Here: Attachment and Reference Are Two Different Things</h2>
<p>This is the most commonly confused distinction.</p>
<h3 id="route-attachment-to-gateway--listener">Route Attachment to Gateway / Listener</h3>
<p>This is governed by:</p>
<ul>
<li>The route's <code>parentRefs</code></li>
<li>The listener's <code>allowedRoutes</code></li>
</ul>
<p>In other words, whether a route can attach to a listener isn't determined by <code>ReferenceGrant</code> — it's determined by whether the listener is willing to accept it.</p>
<h3 id="cross-namespace-resource-references">Cross-Namespace Resource References</h3>
<p>This is where <code>ReferenceGrant</code> typically comes in.
For example:</p>
<ul>
<li>A <code>Gateway</code> wants to reference a <code>Secret</code> in another namespace</li>
<li>An <code>HTTPRoute</code> wants to reference a <code>Service</code> in another namespace</li>
</ul>
<p>This isn't a question of attachment — it's "has the target namespace formally authorized you to use its resources?"</p>
<p>Many people collapse these two into one and start questioning their sanity. The rules aren't that mysterious once the roles are separated.</p>
<h2 id="allowedroutes-decide-who-can-attach-first"><code>allowedRoutes</code>: Decide Who Can Attach First</h2>
<p>The most restrictive listener looks like this:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">apiVersion</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">metadata</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-gateway</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">infra</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">spec</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">        namespaces</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">          from</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">Same</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>This means only routes in the <code>infra</code> namespace can attach to this listener.</p>
<p>To allow specific team namespaces to attach, use <code>Selector</code>:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">allowedRoutes</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespaces</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">    from</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">Selector</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">    selector</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      matchLabels</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>

<p>This pattern works well for platform teams.
You don't have to lock everything down completely, but you also don't have to open it with <code>from: All</code> and let everyone in unrestricted. You can require namespaces to earn the right to attach by carrying a specific label.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 <code>allowedRoutes</code> is per-listener, not per-Gateway. Different listeners can have different access policies.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>If a route or gateway wants to reference resources in another namespace, the target namespace has to authorize it.</p>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">spec</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>

<p>In plain English:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>HTTPRoute</code> resources in <code>team-a</code></li>
<li>Are authorized to reference <code>Service</code> resources in <code>shared-services</code></li>
</ul>
<p>Note that the authorization is <strong>created by the target namespace, not the source</strong>.
The source can't just declare it wants access — the resource owner decides. This design is actually quite principled, because owners should control who accesses their resources.</p>
<h2 id="common-platformapp-team-split">Common Platform/App Team Split</h2>
<p>The most common ownership model looks like this:</p>
<h3 id="platform-team-owns">Platform Team Owns</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>GatewayClass</code></li>
<li><code>Gateway</code></li>
<li><code>Listener</code></li>
<li>TLS certificate management</li>
<li><code>allowedRoutes</code> policies</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="app-teams-own">App Teams Own</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>HTTPRoute</code> / <code>GRPCRoute</code> in their own namespaces</li>
<li>Backend services</li>
<li>Routing rule adjustments</li>
</ul>
<p>The benefits of this split are clear:</p>
<ul>
<li>Platform team guards the ingress exposure and security boundary</li>
<li>App teams can still move fast on their own traffic rules</li>
<li>No one needs to touch the shared Gateway just to change a path</li>
</ul>
<p>You can also give all permissions to one group — that's not wrong.
But in larger teams, that model tends to evolve into "anyone can change it, so when it breaks no one knows who did what."</p>
<h2 id="practical-advice-how-to-avoid-multi-tenant-disasters">Practical Advice: How to Avoid Multi-Tenant Disasters</h2>
<p>Genuinely useful habits:</p>
<ul>
<li>Shared Gateways default to <code>allowedRoutes.namespaces.from: Selector</code></li>
<li>Namespace label conventions should be stable — don't change <code>team-a</code> to <code>allow-gw</code> next month</li>
<li>All cross-namespace backend/Secret references go through <code>ReferenceGrant</code></li>
<li>Route names should indicate team or service — e.g., <code>team-a-api-route</code></li>
<li>Shared listeners should always use <code>sectionName</code> to prevent routes attaching to the wrong entry</li>
</ul>
<p>One principle worth keeping:</p>
<p><strong>If you can enforce a boundary at the ingress layer, don't rely on documentation and hoping people self-govern.</strong></p>
<p>In engineering, process beats verbal agreement. That sounds like advice from someone much older, but it's genuinely useful.</p>
<h2 id="one-line-summary">One-Line Summary</h2>
<p>In Gateway API, <code>allowedRoutes</code> controls "who can enter," and <code>ReferenceGrant</code> controls "what they can access once inside."
Keep those two straight, and multi-namespace and multi-team scenarios become much more manageable.</p>

29:T37e6,<p>One of Gateway API's biggest values isn't just prettier YAML — it finally answers a long-standing question clearly:</p>
<p><strong>Who owns the ingress layer? Who can change routes? Can namespaces cross-reference each other?</strong></p>
<p>Many Ingress pain points came from everything being tangled together.
Platform teams feared unauthorized exposure changes. App teams felt like they had to file a ticket just to change a path. Everyone ended up torturing each other in YAML.</p>
<p>Gateway API's approach is to say clearly:</p>
<ul>
<li>Platform ingress can be independently governed</li>
<li>Routes can be delegated to app teams</li>
<li>Cross-namespace behavior requires explicit authorization</li>
</ul>
<p>This post explains that ownership model from top to bottom.</p>
<h2 id="start-here-attachment-and-reference-are-two-different-things">Start Here: Attachment and Reference Are Two Different Things</h2>
<p>This is the most commonly confused distinction.</p>
<h3 id="route-attachment-to-gateway--listener">Route Attachment to Gateway / Listener</h3>
<p>This is governed by:</p>
<ul>
<li>The route's <code>parentRefs</code></li>
<li>The listener's <code>allowedRoutes</code></li>
</ul>
<p>In other words, whether a route can attach to a listener isn't determined by <code>ReferenceGrant</code> — it's determined by whether the listener is willing to accept it.</p>
<h3 id="cross-namespace-resource-references">Cross-Namespace Resource References</h3>
<p>This is where <code>ReferenceGrant</code> typically comes in.
For example:</p>
<ul>
<li>A <code>Gateway</code> wants to reference a <code>Secret</code> in another namespace</li>
<li>An <code>HTTPRoute</code> wants to reference a <code>Service</code> in another namespace</li>
</ul>
<p>This isn't a question of attachment — it's "has the target namespace formally authorized you to use its resources?"</p>
<p>Many people collapse these two into one and start questioning their sanity. The rules aren't that mysterious once the roles are separated.</p>
<h2 id="allowedroutes-decide-who-can-attach-first"><code>allowedRoutes</code>: Decide Who Can Attach First</h2>
<p>The most restrictive listener looks like this:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">apiVersion</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">kind</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">Gateway</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">metadata</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-gateway</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">infra</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">spec</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  gatewayClassName</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">eg</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  listeners</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">https</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      protocol</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">HTTPS</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      port</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#79C0FF">443</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      allowedRoutes</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">        namespaces</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">          from</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">Same</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>This means only routes in the <code>infra</code> namespace can attach to this listener.</p>
<p>To allow specific team namespaces to attach, use <code>Selector</code>:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">allowedRoutes</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespaces</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">    from</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">Selector</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">    selector</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      matchLabels</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">        shared-gateway-access</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"true"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>This pattern works well for platform teams.
You don't have to lock everything down completely, but you also don't have to open it with <code>from: All</code> and let everyone in unrestricted. You can require namespaces to earn the right to attach by carrying a specific label.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 <code>allowedRoutes</code> is per-listener, not per-Gateway. Different listeners can have different access policies.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="referencegrant-the-target-namespace-decides"><code>ReferenceGrant</code>: The Target Namespace Decides</h2>
<p>If a route or gateway wants to reference resources in another namespace, the target namespace has to authorize it.</p>
<p>For example, an app team's <code>HTTPRoute</code> wants to send traffic to a backend in a shared namespace:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">apiVersion</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">kind</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">HTTPRoute</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">metadata</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">app-route</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">team-a</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">spec</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  parentRefs</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-gateway</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">infra</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      sectionName</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">https</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  rules</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">backendRefs</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">        - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-api</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">          namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-services</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">          port</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#79C0FF">8080</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>The <code>shared-services</code> namespace would need a <code>ReferenceGrant</code>:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">apiVersion</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">gateway.networking.k8s.io/v1beta1</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">kind</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">ReferenceGrant</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">metadata</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  name</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">allow-team-a-route</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">shared-services</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">spec</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  from</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">group</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">gateway.networking.k8s.io</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      kind</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">HTTPRoute</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">      namespace</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">team-a</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#7EE787">  to</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    - </span><span style="color:#7EE787">group</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">""</span></span>

<p>In plain English:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>HTTPRoute</code> resources in <code>team-a</code></li>
<li>Are authorized to reference <code>Service</code> resources in <code>shared-services</code></li>
</ul>
<p>Note that the authorization is <strong>created by the target namespace, not the source</strong>.
The source can't just declare it wants access — the resource owner decides. This design is actually quite principled, because owners should control who accesses their resources.</p>
<h2 id="common-platformapp-team-split">Common Platform/App Team Split</h2>
<p>The most common ownership model looks like this:</p>
<h3 id="platform-team-owns">Platform Team Owns</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>GatewayClass</code></li>
<li><code>Gateway</code></li>
<li><code>Listener</code></li>
<li>TLS certificate management</li>
<li><code>allowedRoutes</code> policies</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="app-teams-own">App Teams Own</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>HTTPRoute</code> / <code>GRPCRoute</code> in their own namespaces</li>
<li>Backend services</li>
<li>Routing rule adjustments</li>
</ul>
<p>The benefits of this split are clear:</p>
<ul>
<li>Platform team guards the ingress exposure and security boundary</li>
<li>App teams can still move fast on their own traffic rules</li>
<li>No one needs to touch the shared Gateway just to change a path</li>
</ul>
<p>You can also give all permissions to one group — that's not wrong.
But in larger teams, that model tends to evolve into "anyone can change it, so when it breaks no one knows who did what."</p>
<h2 id="practical-advice-how-to-avoid-multi-tenant-disasters">Practical Advice: How to Avoid Multi-Tenant Disasters</h2>
<p>Genuinely useful habits:</p>
<ul>
<li>Shared Gateways default to <code>allowedRoutes.namespaces.from: Selector</code></li>
<li>Namespace label conventions should be stable — don't change <code>team-a</code> to <code>allow-gw</code> next month</li>
<li>All cross-namespace backend/Secret references go through <code>ReferenceGrant</code></li>
<li>Route names should indicate team or service — e.g., <code>team-a-api-route</code></li>
<li>Shared listeners should always use <code>sectionName</code> to prevent routes attaching to the wrong entry</li>
</ul>
<p>One principle worth keeping:</p>
<p><strong>If you can enforce a boundary at the ingress layer, don't rely on documentation and hoping people self-govern.</strong></p>
<p>In engineering, process beats verbal agreement. That sounds like advice from someone much older, but it's genuinely useful.</p>
<h2 id="one-line-summary">One-Line Summary</h2>
<p>In Gateway API, <code>allowedRoutes</code> controls "who can enter," and <code>ReferenceGrant</code> controls "what they can access once inside."
Keep those two straight, and multi-namespace and multi-team scenarios become much more manageable.</p>
