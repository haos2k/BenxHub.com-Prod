<h2 id="four-tool-design-rules">Four tool design rules</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Do one thing only</strong>: if it searches, do not also write files</li>
<li><strong>Be explicit</strong>: <code>description</code> should clearly state purpose and input</li>
<li><strong>Validate parameters</strong>: use Pydantic schema</li>
<li><strong>Return stable format</strong>: easier for downstream tasks to parse</li>
</ol>
