3:Te87,<p>By default, many agents finish a run and behave like they forgot everything on the next one.<br>
For continuous workflows, memory and knowledge sources are essential.</p>
<h2 id="separate-these-two-capabilities-first">Separate these two capabilities first</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>memory</code>: in-flow or long-term interaction context</li>
<li><code>knowledge_sources</code>: external documents (PDFs, documentation sets, etc.)</li>
</ul>
<p>In short: one is "remember what we just discussed," the other is "know what the manual says."</p>
