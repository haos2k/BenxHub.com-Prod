3:T1191,<p>Three modes, one sentence to tell them apart: <strong>who can touch your code, and who can only talk</strong>. Once you get that, picking the right mode is easy.</p>
<h2 id="one-sentence-comparison">One-Sentence Comparison</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Mode</th>
<th>One Sentence</th>
<th>Does it modify your files?</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Agent</strong></td>
<td>"Do" something for me</td>
<td>✅ Yes</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Plan</strong></td>
<td>"Plan" how to do it first, then I'll decide</td>
<td>Not yet — only after you approve</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Ask</strong></td>
<td>Just "ask" a question or look something up</td>
<td>❌ No</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>Think of them as three colleagues: Agent is the one who says "hand it to me, I'll just do it"; Plan is the one who says "let me write up a proposal for you to review first"; Ask is the one who says "I'll only answer, I won't touch the files" — pick the right person and save yourself half the trouble.</p>
<h2 id="agent-mode-default">Agent Mode (Default)</h2>
<p><strong>Use case</strong>: Have the AI directly edit code, add features, or refactor. You want results, not a discussion.</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFA657">cursor</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> agent</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> "Change login to use JWT"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<ul>
<li>Reads files, edits files, may run commands</li>
<li>You can accept or reject each step — you're in control</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="plan-mode">Plan Mode</h2>
<p><strong>Use case</strong>: Not sure how to approach something, or worried about the AI making too many changes at once? See the plan before giving the go-ahead.</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFA657">cursor</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> agent</span><span style="color:#79C0FF"> --plan</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> "Refactor the entire API layer"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<ul>
<li>The AI will ask a few questions and propose an approach</li>
<li>It only acts after you agree — no surprises</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>When to use</strong>: For large changes, or whenever you want to "see it clearly before committing."</p>
<h2 id="ask-mode-read-only">Ask Mode (Read-Only)</h2>
<p><strong>Use case</strong>: You just want to ask "what does this code do" or "is there a better way to write this" — and you absolutely <strong>do not want any files changed</strong>.</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFA657">cursor</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> ask</span><span style="color:#A5D6FF"> "What is the logic in src/auth.ts?"</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<ul>
<li>Only reads, analyzes, and answers — never writes</li>
<li>Great for CI, or environments where you only want to inspect, not modify</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="switching-modes-mid-conversation">Switching Modes Mid-Conversation</h2>
<p>Already inside a <code>cursor agent</code> conversation? Use <strong>slash commands</strong> to switch instantly:</p>
<ul>
<li><code>/plan</code> → Switch to Plan mode (plan first, act later)</li>
<li><code>/ask</code> → Switch to Ask mode (read-only, ask freely)</li>
</ul>
<p>No need to exit and restart — it's like switching gears on the fly.</p>
<h2 id="summary">Summary</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Agent</strong>: gets things done, modifies files — use this when you want results</li>
<li><strong>Plan</strong>: plans first, acts only after you approve — use this when you want safety</li>
<li><strong>Ask</strong>: questions only, read-only — use this when you want answers without changes</li>
</ul>
