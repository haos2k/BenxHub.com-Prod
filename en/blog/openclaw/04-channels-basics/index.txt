1b:T189c,<p>If your assistant only works in a browser, how different is that from chatting on a normal AI website?</p>
<p><strong>Channels</strong> are the answer: they let your assistant live inside the apps you already use every day.</p>
<h2 id="what-youll-learn">What You'll Learn</h2>
<ul>
<li>Understand what a Channel is</li>
<li>Set up a Telegram bot</li>
<li>Control how your assistant talks inside groups</li>
<li>See what becomes possible with multiple channels</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="what-is-a-channel">What Is a Channel?</h2>
<p>Think of a Channel as <strong>one of several doors</strong> into the same assistant:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>WebChat</strong>: the browser door, already installed for you</li>
<li><strong>Telegram Bot</strong>: the phone door</li>
<li><strong>WhatsApp</strong>: another door</li>
<li><strong>Slack, Discord, iMessage...</strong>: even more doors</li>
</ul>
<p>The same assistant can have many doors open at once. No matter which door you use, you still reach the same assistant.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="webchat-ready-to-use-without-extra-setup">WebChat: Ready to Use Without Extra Setup</h2>
<p>Open the dashboard, find the <strong>Chat</strong> tab on the left, pick an agent, and start chatting. This is the easiest channel and needs no extra configuration.</p>
<p>It is also perfect for confirming that your assistant is working before you wire up other apps.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="set-up-a-telegram-bot">Set Up a Telegram Bot</h2>
<h3 id="step-1-create-the-bot-with-botfather">Step 1: Create the Bot with BotFather</h3>
<ol>
<li>Open Telegram and search for <code>@BotFather</code></li>
<li>Send <code>/newbot</code></li>
<li>Follow the prompts to set the bot name</li>
<li>Copy your <strong>bot token</strong> such as <code>123456:ABC-DEF1234ghIkl-zyx57W2v1u123ew11</code></li>
</ol>
<h3 id="step-2-configure-channelstelegram-in-openclaw">Step 2: Configure <code>channels.telegram</code> in OpenClaw</h3>
<p>Open the dashboard → Config → find <code>channels.telegram</code> and fill in something like:</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#0d1117;color:#e6edf3" tabindex="0" data-language="json5" data-theme="github-dark-default"><code data-language="json5" data-theme="github-dark-default" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">{</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">  "channels"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: {</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">    "telegram"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: {</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "enabled"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#79C0FF">true</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "botToken"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"paste your bot token here"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "dmPolicy"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"pairing"</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    }</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">  }</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">}</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<h3 id="step-3-test-it">Step 3: Test It</h3>
<p>Search for your bot on Telegram and send "hello". Your assistant should respond.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>⚠️ The first DM may require pairing. Just follow the instructions from the bot reply.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="how-should-groups-be-configured">How Should Groups Be Configured?</h2>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "enabled"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#79C0FF">true</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "botToken"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"your bot token"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">      "dmPolicy"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"pairing"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">,</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#A5D6FF">        "*"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: { </span><span style="color:#A5D6FF">"requireMention"</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3">: </span><span style="color:#79C0FF">true</span><span style="color:#E6EDF3"> }</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E6EDF3">    }</span></span>

<p>Now the assistant only speaks when someone explicitly mentions the bot.</p>
