<h2 id="what-are-cron-and-heartbeat">What Are Cron and Heartbeat?</h2>
<p><strong>Cron</strong> means "do this thing at this time," like writing a calendar for your assistant:</p>
<ul>
<li>Every day at 9 AM: summarize yesterday's emails</li>
<li>Every Monday: remind me what matters this week</li>
<li>Every night: archive today's notes automatically</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Heartbeat</strong> means the assistant wakes up at a fixed interval, such as every 30 minutes, checks whether something needs doing, and acts if necessary.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 If you see a heartbeat log every 30 minutes, that is normal.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="configure-cron-in-the-dashboard">Configure Cron in the Dashboard</h2>
<p>Open Control UI → <strong>Cron</strong> tab:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Add schedule</strong>: enter a Cron expression and the task description</li>
<li><strong>Enable / disable</strong>: pause any job at any time</li>
<li><strong>Run now</strong>: test a schedule immediately without waiting</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="cron-syntax-quick-reference">Cron Syntax Quick Reference</h2>
<pre><code>* * * * *
│ │ │ │ └─ day of week (0-7, both 0 and 7 mean Sunday)
│ │ │ └─── month (1-12)
│ │ └───── day of month (1-31)
│ └─────── hour (0-23)
└───────── minute (0-59)
</code></pre>
<p>Common examples:</p>
<pre><code>0 9 * * *      ← every day at 9:00
0 9 * * 1      ← every Monday at 9:00
0 */6 * * *    ← every 6 hours
</code></pre>

<h2 id="cron-job-never-triggered-check-these-first">Cron Job Never Triggered? Check These First</h2>
<ol>
<li>Is Cron enabled in Config?</li>
<li>Is the timezone correct?</li>
<li>Do the logs show an error? Run <code>openclaw logs --follow</code></li>
<li>Try restarting the Gateway</li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>⚠️ Scheduled jobs also consume model resources. If you run many schedules and lots of chats at once, you may hit rate limits such as HTTP 429. Heavy jobs may deserve their own dedicated agent.</p>
</blockquote>
