3:Td2a,<p>Your assistant should not go offline just because your laptop decided to take a nap.</p>
<p>This article shows how to move the Gateway to the cloud and keep your assistant online all day.</p>

<li>Know where a Gateway can run</li>
<li>Complete a basic VPS deployment</li>

<li>Use Tailscale to build a safe private network</li>
<li>Understand the difference between Gateway and Node</li>
</ul>
<hr>

<h2 id="what-does-vps-installation-look-like">What Does VPS Installation Look Like?</h2>
<p>Log into your VPS, preferably Ubuntu or Debian, then follow the Linux installation steps from <a href="https://docs.openclaw.ai">docs.openclaw.ai</a>. It is almost the same as a local install, just happening on a remote machine instead.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="remote-authentication-basics">Remote Authentication Basics</h2>
<p>Once the Gateway is on a VPS, OpenClaw still needs to confirm that you are an authorized user:</p>
<ul>
<li>You must use a token or device pairing</li>
<li>If you see <code>unauthorized</code>, check <code>gateway.bind</code> and confirm the token is correct</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2 id="tailscale-is-the-cleanest-option">Tailscale Is the Cleanest Option</h2>
<p><strong>Tailscale</strong> is a VPN that makes your phone, computer, and VPS feel like they are on the same private network:</p>
<ol>
<li>Install Tailscale on both your VPS and your own computer</li>
<li>Set <code>gateway.bind: tailnet</code></li>
<li>Connect to the Gateway through Tailscale from any device</li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 Tailscale can also expose HTTPS with Serve, which is usually much safer than exposing a raw port directly.</p>
</blockquote>
<hr>
<h2 id="what-is-a-node">What Is a Node?</h2>
<p>Here is the cool part: <strong>Gateway and Node can live on different machines</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gateway</strong>: runs on the VPS and handles conversations, memory, Telegram, and all the "brain" work</li>
<li><strong>Node</strong>: runs on your home computer and provides the "hands" such as browser control, local file access, and screen interaction</li>
</ul>
<p>Real-world example: while you are outside, you message your assistant on Telegram and ask it to open a browser on your home PC and check a website. The Gateway sends the task to the Node at home, then returns the result to you.</p>
<p><strong>How is this different from SSH?</strong></p>
<p>With SSH, you log in and type the commands yourself. With a Node, <strong>the assistant does it for you</strong>, fully integrated with Skills, workspaces, and conversations.</p>
