<h2 id="why-should-you-care">Why Should You Care?</h2>
<p>Imagine hiring an incredibly capable personal assistant, but with one strange condition: <strong>they have to live in someone else's house</strong>. Every task, every conversation, and every piece of data must pass through that third party first.</p>
<p>That is basically how most cloud AI assistants work today.</p>
<p><strong>OpenClaw</strong> aims to flip that around: bring that assistant back into your own home.</p>
<hr>
<h2 id="what-is-openclaw">What Is OpenClaw?</h2>
<p><strong>Short version</strong>: OpenClaw is a "local-first" AI assistant control center that runs on your own computer or server. It lets you talk to your assistant from Telegram, WhatsApp, the web UI, and other apps you already use, while <strong>keeping your data and workspace under your control</strong>.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 "Local-first" does not mean "never touches the internet." It means <strong>you stay in charge</strong>: where the data lives, which model you use, and who gets access.</p>
</blockquote>
