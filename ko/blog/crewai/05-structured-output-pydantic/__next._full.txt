<li>실패를 더 빨리 발견 가능(필드 불일치 시 즉시 오류)</li>
</ul>
<p>즉 API 계약과 같습니다. 계약이 있어야 협업이 안정됩니다.</p>
<h2 id="출력-모델-만들기">출력 모델 만들기</h2>

<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#F97583">class</span><span style="color:#B392F0"> Finding</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">(</span><span style="color:#B392F0">BaseModel</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">):</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    title: </span><span style="color:#79B8FF">str</span><span style="color:#F97583"> =</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> Field(</span><span style="color:#FFAB70">description</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"Finding title"</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    summary: </span><span style="color:#79B8FF">str</span><span style="color:#F97583"> =</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> Field(</span><span style="color:#FFAB70">description</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"Brief explanation"</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    source: </span><span style="color:#79B8FF">str</span><span style="color:#F97583"> =</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> Field(</span><span style="color:#FFAB70">description</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"Reference URL"</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span>

<h2 id="task에서-output_pydantic-사용">Task에서 <code>output_pydantic</code> 사용</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="python" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="python" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#F97583">from</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> crewai </span><span style="color:#F97583">import</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> Task</span></span>
<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">research_task </span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8"> Task(</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFAB70">    description</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"Research AI observability tools in 2026."</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFAB70">    expected_output</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">"Structured research result."</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#FFAB70">    output_pydantic</span><span style="color:#F97583">=</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">ResearchReport,</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">)</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>실행 후에는 텍스트를 추측해 파싱하지 않고 안정적인 필드 접근이 가능합니다.</p>
<h2 id="함께-쓰면-좋은-방식">함께 쓰면 좋은 방식</h2>
<ul>
<li><code>expected_output</code>에 품질 요구를 계속 명시</li>
<li>필드 의미를 구체화(<code>summary</code> 길이, <code>source</code> 형식)</li>
<li>후속 Task는 문체가 아니라 필드에 의존</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="초보가-자주-하는-실수">초보가 자주 하는 실수</h2>
<ol>
<li>Pydantic이 있으면 <code>expected_output</code>이 필요 없다고 생각함</li>
<li>필드가 너무 적어서 정보가 부족함</li>
<li>필드가 너무 많고 세밀해서 누락이 늘어남</li>
</ol>
<blockquote>
<p>⚠️ 먼저 핵심 3~5개 필드로 시작하고, 필요할 때 확장하세요.</p>
</blockquote>
