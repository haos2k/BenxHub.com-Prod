<h2 id="étape-4--configurer-les-tâches">Étape 4 : Configurer les tâches</h2>
<p>Modifiez <code>tasks.yaml</code> :</p>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="yaml" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">research_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  description</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#9ECBFF">    Research {topic}. Focus on trends, tools, and practical examples in 2026.</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  expected_output</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#F97583">></span></span>

<span data-line=""> </span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">report_task</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">:</span></span>

<span data-line=""><span style="color:#E1E4E8">    - </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">research_task</span></span>
<span data-line=""><span style="color:#85E89D">  output_file</span><span style="color:#E1E4E8">: </span><span style="color:#9ECBFF">output/report.md</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<blockquote>
<p>💡 Le <code>context</code> est important : il indique à la deuxième tâche de consommer les résultats de la première.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="étape-5--lancer-le-crew">Étape 5 : Lancer le crew</h2>
<figure data-rehype-pretty-code-figure=""><pre style="background-color:#24292e;color:#e1e4e8" tabindex="0" data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark"><code data-language="bash" data-theme="github-dark" style="display: grid;"><span data-line=""><span style="color:#B392F0">crewai</span><span style="color:#9ECBFF"> run</span></span></code></pre></figure>
<p>En cas de succès, vous verrez le résultat dans <code>output/report.md</code>.<br>
Un délai au premier lancement est normal. Les appels au modèle, c'est comme un chat qui se réveille : il faut un moment avant qu'il ne réagisse.</p>
